# Margined privacy policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Margined is a local Markdown app for macOS, iPhone, and iPad, developed by HighGainDesign. It does not require an account and does not upload your documents to a HighGainDesign server. Margined has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

## Your files and settings
Margined reads files you choose. When you save checkmarks or discussions, it writes those changes to your Markdown file. Saved discussions include the author labels, dates, messages, and passage information in that file; sharing the file can share those notes too. Preferences, remembered folder permissions, and reading progress are stored on your device. Saved speech progress uses hashes and offsets rather than copies of document text. Operating-system backups and any storage provider you choose, such as iCloud Drive or Dropbox, are governed by your settings and that provider’s practices.

## Rendering, speech, and local streaming
Markdown, math, and diagrams are rendered on your device. Read Aloud uses installed Apple voices; Margined does not send the text to a model service. On Mac, Live Streaming reads the named local Marked-compatible preview pasteboard while you use that feature; it does not use a streaming server or read the general clipboard for this purpose.

## Connections you choose
Remote images are blocked by default. If you choose Load Remote Images for a document, Margined allows HTTPS requests to the image hosts specified in that document. Those hosts can receive your IP address and normal connection information. Margined requests those images without a document referrer. The choice resets when you close the document; Quick Look previews remain offline. Opening a web or email link hands the link to your browser or the relevant app. Exporting, sharing, or printing sends the selected content to the destination you choose. Those destinations have their own privacy practices.

## TestFlight and feedback
Apple distributes TestFlight builds and may provide crash reports, diagnostics, or feedback under Apple’s TestFlight terms and your device settings. If you send us feedback or email, we receive the information and attachments you choose to include and use them to investigate problems and respond. Avoid including private document content unless you intend to share it with us.

## Your choices and contact
You control the Markdown files and storage locations you choose. You can manage remembered folder permissions in Margined Settings. Removing Margined does not remove documents stored elsewhere. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to dev@highgain.design.
